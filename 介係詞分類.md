# 介係詞分類
1. 時間 (when)
2. 空間 (where)
3. 方向 (where to)
4. 原因 (why)
5. 關係 (relation)

## 1️⃣ 時間（Time）
### 時間點
	at (at 5pm / at night / at noon)
	on (on Monday / on April 10th)
	in (in 2026 / in April / in the morning)
### 時間範圍
	during
	for
	since
	until / till
	from … to / until
	by (by 5pm = 最遲)
	before
	after
### 時間方向類 (具有參考點的範圍)
	ago
	later
	prior to
	following
### 持續 / 覆蓋
	throughout
	over (over the years)
	within


## 2️⃣ 空間（Space / Location）
### 基本位置
	in
	on
	at
### 內外關係
	inside
	outside
	within
	without
### 上下
	above
	below
	over
	under
	beneath
	underneath
### 前後
	in front of
	behind
	before (空間用法)
	after (空間用法)
### 旁邊 / 鄰近
	beside
	next to
	near
	close to
	far from
### 包圍 / 群體
	among (三者以上)
	between (兩者)
	around
	throughout
### 穿越 / 經過空間
	through
	across
	along
	past

## 3️⃣ 方向（Direction / Movement）
👉 回答「往哪裡移動」
### 基本方向
	to
	from
	toward(s)
### 進入 / 離開
	into
	onto
	out of
	off
### 上下移動
	up
	down
### 穿越 / 經過
	through
	across
	along
	past
### 路徑 / 中轉
	via
	around
	over (飛越/跨越)

## 4️⃣ 原因（Cause / Reason）
👉 TOEFL 閱讀 & 聽力超高頻
### 直接原因
	because of
	due to
	owing to
### 正式學術
	as a result of
	on account of
### 正向原因（好結果）
	thanks to
### 內在原因 / 心理原因
	out of (fear / curiosity / interest / respect)
### 結果導向
	for (He was punished for cheating)


## 5️⃣ 關係（Relation / Abstract / Logical）
👉 TOEFL 最重要大類（學術句型核心）
### 所屬 / 結構
	of (the roof of the house)
### 使用 / 伴隨
	with
	without
	by (by car / by email / by Shakespeare)
### 主題 / 內容
	about
	regarding
	concerning
	in regard to
	with regard to
### 比較 / 對比
	like
	unlike
	as
	than (比較級結構)
### 替代 / 排除
	instead of
	except for
	besides
	apart from
	### 包含 / 範圍
	including
	within
	among
### 根據 / 依據
	according to
	based on
	in terms of
	in view of
### 抽象關係（TOEFL超重要）
	in response to
	in relation to
	in connection with
	in addition to
	in contrast to
	in spite of / despite
